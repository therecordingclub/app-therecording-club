A benefit dinner for Tiny Room Gives.

Why we are here

The opportunity to empower.

Tiny Room Gives is more than a scholarship fund. It is a lifeline for emerging artists who dream of making music their career. Through financial support, mentorship, and access to world-class recording facilities, we give young creatives the chance to be heard, to grow, and to thrive.

At this year's dinner, our scholarship musicians take the spotlight, performing alongside a special guest headliner, sharing their stories, and showing firsthand the transformative power of opportunity. Every dollar raised welcomes more scholars into The Recording Club family.

A few of our scholarship musicians

The artists you're supporting.

Budda Foster

A Los Angeles multi-instrumentalist who has performed with Snoh Aalegra, Dominic Fike, Cordae, Queen Naija, The Game, and Durand Bernarr, on both guitar and bass.

Devin Daniels

Inglewood-born alto saxophonist and Herb Alpert Fellow. Toured the US with Herbie Hancock in 2024 following the Hollywood Bowl tribute to Wayne Shorter.

Jimetta Rose

Los Angeles-born singer, songwriter, and poet whose work blends jazz, R&B, and hip-hop. Has collaborated with Erykah Badu, Talib Kweli, and Bilal.
